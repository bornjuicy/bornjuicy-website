Good to know

Can I get Born Juicy delivered today?

Yes. Order on GrabFood: search “Born Juicy - Geylang Bahru Ville”. For larger or scheduled orders, WhatsApp us at +65 9679 1309.

How early should we order CNY gifts?

Order by 12 December 2026 for delivery between 18 January and 3 February 2027. Slots are limited, so earlier is better, especially for 50 gifts or more.

Can you put our logo on the gifts?

Yes. Every box can carry a printed greeting card with your logo and message, free from 20 gifts.

How do payment and invoicing work?

You get one written quote. A 50% deposit confirms your order or event date, and the balance is due on delivery. We take PayNow and bank transfer and issue one invoice.
